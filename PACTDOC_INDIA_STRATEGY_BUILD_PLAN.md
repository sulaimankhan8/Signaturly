# PactDoc India Strategy & Build Plan

Oct 11, 2026 · @sulaiman khan

## Strategy in one page

PactDoc should become the self-serve signing tool for India's small organisations: pay in rupees, sign in your own language, start from a ready template for your trade, and add Aadhaar eSign when the document needs it.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who is it for first? | Landlords and tenants, housing societies, small employers, accountants and brokers |
| Who comes later? | Small lenders and co-operative credit societies, then banks |
| What do they buy? | A document sent, signed and sealed in minutes, from a template built for Indian use |
| Why PactDoc and not Docusign or Zoho Sign? | Rupee pay-per-use pricing, Indian templates, regional languages, WhatsApp delivery |
| Why PactDoc and not Leegality or Digio? | No sales call, public prices, usable by a five-person office |
| How does it make money? | Monthly plans by seat, plus per-use fees for Aadhaar eSign, SMS and extra documents |
| What must be true first? | The security and evidence fixes in the audit are done |

The plan has three layers, built in this order:

1. **A trustworthy core.** Secure storage, enforced signer checks, a sealed PDF and a complete audit log.
2. **India fit.** Indian-law templates by industry, Hindi and regional scripts, WhatsApp links, UPI billing.
3. **Legal strength on demand.** Aadhaar eSign and e-stamping through licensed partners, sold per use.

One caution up front: banks will not buy from an uncertified solo vendor. This plan reaches them last, through smaller lenders first. The companion documents are the Product & Technology Audit and the Business & Market Report.

## Market map: edges, gaps and how to fill them

Three groups sell e-signatures in India, and none of them is built for a small office that wants to start today without talking to sales.

**Size of the audience.** Over 8.84 crore enterprises were registered on the Udyam portals on 30 June 2026, per a [Rajya Sabha reply reported by KNN](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/msme/over-884-crore-msmes-registered-on-udyam-platforms-mos-karandlaje). Almost all are micro enterprises. This is the pool of possible users, not of buyers; how many would pay is unknown and is the first thing to test.

### Who is there today and where each is strong

| Group | Examples | Their edge | Their weak spot for small customers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Indian compliance vendors | Leegality, Digio, eMudhra, SignDesk | Aadhaar eSign, e-stamping, links to lenders' systems, regulator-grade security | Sold by sales teams; prices not public; built for lenders and insurers |
| Global self-serve tools | Docusign, Dropbox Sign, Zoho Sign, signNow | Polished products, integrations, brand trust | Dollar per-seat pricing; US-style templates; English-first; email-first |
| Open-source tools | Documenso, DocuSeal | Free to self-host; cheap API | Need a developer; no Indian legal features |
| Informal habits | Print, sign, photograph, send on WhatsApp | Free and familiar | No proof of who signed or that the file is unchanged |

The fourth row is the real competitor for most small organisations. PactDoc wins when it is nearly as easy as WhatsApp and clearly safer.

### PactDoc's own edges to protect

- **Bulk send from a spreadsheet** is already built and suits societies, schools and HR.
- **Public verification** already exists and becomes a strong trust feature once the PDF is sealed.
- **A solo-founder cost base** allows prices that sales-led vendors cannot match.

### Where not to compete

Do not chase large banks, insurers or enterprises yet. They require security certifications, data-centre commitments and procurement cycles that the larger vendors already satisfy.

### What makes a template worth paying for

- **Fill-in questions, not a blank PDF.** The sender answers ten questions and the document is generated with names, amounts and dates in place.
- **Roles and fields already placed.** Landlord, tenant and two witnesses arrive pre-assigned.
- **A signature-type hint.** Each template states whether ordinary e-sign is usually enough, whether Aadhaar eSign is advised, and whether stamping or registration applies.
- **State notes.** Rent and property templates carry short notes for the main states, starting with Maharashtra, Karnataka, Delhi, Tamil Nadu and Telangana.
- **Languages.** English first, then Hindi, then Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati and Bengali for the rental and society packs.
- **A named reviewer and a date.** "Reviewed by an advocate, October 2026" builds more trust than any feature.

### How to produce them

1. Hire one Indian contracts lawyer on a per-template fee to draft or review each pack.
2. Launch with Starter, Rentals and Housing society, about 29 templates.
3. Add one pack a month, chosen by what customers upload most.
4. Put a disclaimer on every template: it is a starting draft, not legal advice.
5. Let Enterprise customers upload their own templates and lock them for their team.

## Law and compliance in India

Ordinary e-signatures are valid for most everyday contracts in India, but some documents need a stronger signature, stamp duty or registration, and a few cannot be signed electronically at all. This section is a working summary, not legal advice. Have an Indian lawyer confirm it before the product makes any claim.

### Signature types

| Type | What it is | Standing | PactDoc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary e-signature | Drawn or typed signature with consent, email check and audit trail | Supports a valid contract under the IT Act; the party relying on it must prove who signed | Built, needs the audit fixes |
| Aadhaar eSign | Signer authenticates by Aadhaar OTP or biometric; a licensed provider issues a one-time certificate and signs the document | A recognised electronic signature with stronger legal presumptions | Through a partner |
| Digital signature certificate | A personal certificate on a token, issued by a certifying authority | Same recognised class; required for many government filings | Accept as upload later |

### How Aadhaar eSign works and how to get it

The signer starts in your app, consents and enters their Aadhaar number, and UIDAI authenticates them by OTP or biometric. A licensed eSign provider then has a certifying authority issue a certificate and returns the signed document. [Cashfree's explainer](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/what-is-esign-with-aadhaar/) describes these roles and says about 18 licensed providers exist, including eMudhra, Protean and C-DAC.

There are two ways in:

| Route | What it involves | Suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use an aggregator's API | Sign a commercial contract with a vendor that already holds the provider relationship; integrate their API | PactDoc now |
| Register directly with a licensed provider | An agreement with the provider, an audited signing server and, per secondary sources, hardware | High volume later |

Both need a registered business entity. Start with the aggregator route.

### Documents with special rules

A 2022 government notification narrowed the list of documents excluded from the IT Act. Per [Leegality's explainer](https://leegality.com/blog/first-schedule), contracts for the sale or conveyance of immovable property were removed from the excluded list. Powers of attorney, demand promissory notes and bills of exchange became signable when they favour an entity regulated by RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, NHB or PFRDA.

| Document | Can PactDoc handle it? | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyday commercial, HR and service contracts | Yes | Stamp duty may still apply by state |
| Rent and leave-and-licence agreements | Yes for signing | Stamping applies; some states require registration whatever the term. Secondary sources say Maharashtra does. |
| Loan papers in favour of a regulated lender | Yes, with Aadhaar eSign and stamping | Phase 3 |
| Promissory notes and powers of attorney between private individuals | Treat as not supported | Reported as still excluded |
| Wills and trust deeds | No | I could not confirm their status from the sources read; assume excluded |
| Sale deeds and other registered instruments | No | Registration happens at the sub-registrar or the state portal |

**Stamp duty** is set by each state and applies whether a document is on paper or electronic. PactDoc should say this clearly on affected templates and later offer e-stamping through a partner.

### Evidence in a dispute

Electronic records are admitted in court with a certificate about the system that produced them, under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. The admin panel already generates such a certificate. It only has value if the underlying log is complete and the PDF is sealed, which is why the audit fixes come first.

### Data protection

The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were notified on 13 November 2025, with the main duties starting 18 months later, around May 2027, per [Mondaq's summary](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1708164/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified). PactDoc holds names, emails, IP addresses, signatures and, with Aadhaar eSign, identity data.

| Duty | What PactDoc must do |
| --- | --- |
| Clear notice | Itemise the data collected and why, with a link to withdraw consent and to complain |
| Security safeguards | Encryption, access controls, monitoring, backups, security terms with vendors |
| Log retention | Keep logs for at least one year |
| Breach reporting | Tell affected people and the Board without delay; detailed report within 72 hours |
| Erasure | Delete data when the purpose ends or consent is withdrawn, unless law requires keeping it |

Two product rules follow. Never store Aadhaar numbers; keep only what the provider returns as proof. Host documents in an Indian cloud region, because Indian business customers will ask.

### Selling to lenders

Regulated lenders audit their vendors. Expect requests for a security certification, data storage in India, an incident process and the right to inspect. I did not research the current RBI rules; get the checklist from a first prospect before building for it.
